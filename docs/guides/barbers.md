## Logging in

Open the shop's Next Up address on your phone and log in with the username and password the owner gave you. The first time, you're asked to choose your own password.

Then pick the shop you're working at today. If you have a usual shop, it's at the top as a single button. Once you've picked, you show up on that shop's kiosk, so customers can ask for you by name.

{% include figure.html src="choose-location.webp" size="phone" alt="The location picker on a phone" %}

To open it like an app next time, add it to your home screen: in Safari, tap the Share button and then **Add to Home Screen**; in Chrome on Android, open the menu and tap **Add to Home screen**. On an iPhone the home-screen app keeps its own login, so you log in once more inside it.

## Your screen

The top half is the queue: who's **In the Chair** and who's **Waiting**, in order. It updates by itself as customers check in and other barbers call people, so you never need to refresh.

{% include figure.html src="staff-dashboard.webp" alt="The staff dashboard showing the chair, the waiting list, recently finished customers and the shop controls" %}

### Calling someone up

When your chair is free, tap **Call to Chair** next to the person at the top of the list. They move to In the Chair with your name next to them, and the TV shows it too.

Customers who asked for you are marked **Asked for you**. Someone who asked for another barber says so ("Prefers Priya"). It's up to the shop how strictly you follow that. The app doesn't stop you calling anyone.

### When the cut is done

Tap **Complete**. They drop off the queue and count towards the day's cuts.

### Someone left

If a customer has gone before you called them, tap **Remove** next to their name and confirm. They're counted as a no-show.

### Undo

Tapped Complete or Remove by mistake? Finished customers stay under **Recently Finished** for ten minutes, each with an **Undo** button. Undo puts them back where they were: a completed customer goes back in the chair, a removed one goes back in line.

{% include figure.html src="staff-recent.webp" size="card" alt="Recently Finished, with an Undo button next to each customer" %}

## The shop controls

Below the queue are controls for the shop you're working at. Any barber can use them.

### Closing to new check-ins

**Close Store** stops the kiosk taking names, for example when the queue is already longer than you can get through before closing time. Customers already waiting stay in the queue. **Open Store** starts taking names again.

{% include figure.html src="staff-store-card.webp" size="card" alt="The store card, showing the shop is open, with a Close Store button" %}

If your shop opens and closes on a schedule, the card says when the next change is due. Closing early or opening late by hand holds until the next scheduled opening or closing, or midnight at the latest, so a shop opened late for one last customer won't be left taking names overnight.

The card also warns you if the kiosk has lost its connection. While that note is showing, customers can't check in, so check the tablet.

### Announcements

Type a short notice and tap **Save** to show it on the TV and the kiosk, for example "Card machine is down, cash only today". It clears itself at midnight. Tap **Clear** to take it down sooner.

{% include figure.html src="staff-announcement.webp" size="card" alt="The announcement box with a notice typed in" %}

### Going on break

**Go On Break** takes you off the kiosk's list of barbers, so nobody new asks for you while you're out. You can still call customers if you need to. Tap **Back from Break** when you return. Logging out ends your break too.

{% include figure.html src="staff-break.webp" size="card" alt="The break card with a Go On Break button" %}

## Moving shops and going home

- **Change Location** at the top switches you to another shop. You move off the first shop's kiosk and onto the new one.
- **Profile** is where you change your name as customers see it, your password, and your usual shop.
- **Log out** at the end of the day. You come off the kiosk straight away. If you forget, you're logged out after 12 hours without using the app.

## When something looks wrong

- **A red "Connection lost" bar across the top** means your phone can't reach the app, so the queue isn't updating. It goes away when the connection comes back. Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data.
- **The page changed by itself.** After an update, or when the owner changes the shop's colours or logo, the screens reload on their own. That's normal.
- **Logged out unexpectedly.** The owner may have reset your password or logged you out from the Admin page. Log in again, or ask them.
